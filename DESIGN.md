## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, upscale, and fiercely professional aesthetic for a high-end body art and piercing studio in Osasco. The atmosphere merges artistic passion with clinical sanitation standards, dismantling old-school abrasive tattoo shop stereotypes in favor of an inclusive, reassuring, and curated sanctuary.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Architectural & Editorial:** Layouts favor deliberate spatial pacing, deep inkwells of black, and warm metallic accents reminiscent of jewelry grade titanium and antique gold ink pots.
- **Sterile Confidence Meets Warmth:** Balances clinical rigor (autoclave certification, single-use guarantees, clear aftercare) with human empathy and welcoming reassurance for first-timers and heavily modified collectors alike.
- **Sophisticated Craft:** Visual elements evoke physical craftsmanship—delicate line work, controlled contrasts, and tactile elevation rather than loud gothic tropes or aggressive grunge.

### Design Movement
**Minimalist Noir with Tactile Warmth**: Deep multi-tiered charcoal surfaces, micro-borders with hairline gold fidelity, deliberate typographic tension between classical editorial serifs and functional sans, and disciplined restraint.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep dark mode immersion, balancing rich charcoal foundations with warm metallic highlights and clinical validation accents.

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`) & Secondary (`#E5B869`):** Burnished gold and champagne brass tones. Used deliberately for primary conversion actions, active selection states, studio awards, and fine ornamental accents. Never flooded across large containers; reserved for high-value optical focus.
- **Tertiary (`#50B498`):** Restrained medical jade. Reserved exclusively for hygiene validation markers, sterilized equipment badges, certified artist seals, and healing/aftercare checkpoints to instill absolute physiological safety.
- **Surface Neutrals (`#0D0D11` to `#22222D`):** 
  - `Canvas / Deep Base`: `#0D0D11` (infinite ink baseline)
  - `Surface Container Low`: `#16161D` (base card background)
  - `Surface Container High`: `#1E1E28` (interactive states, hover tiers, elevated modals)
  - `Surface Container Highest`: `#262634` (subtle floating bars, chips, segmented toggles)
- **Border & Hairline System:** `#2A2A38` (default border), `#3D3728` (elevated brass-tinted border for hero treatments).
- **Text & Content Hierarchy:**
  - `Text Prominent`: `#F4F4F6` (95% contrast for effortless readability)
  - `Text Muted`: `#A6A6B4` (secondary metadata, artist bios, specs)
  - `Text Accent`: `#E5B869` (pricing highlights, category callouts, key values)

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial grace of **Newsreader** with the structured, contemporary precision of **Space Grotesk**.

- **Editorial Presence (Newsreader):** Used for all headlines, section declarations, and thematic quotes. The italic variant adds an intimate, personal cadence to artist signatures, customer testimonials, and stylistic genres (e.g., *Fine Line*, *Micro-realism*, *Curated Piercing*).
- **Technical Precision (Space Grotesk):** Provides crisp geometry and legibility for body copy, specs, pricing tables, booking forms, and sterile protocol instructions.
- **Stylistic Conventions:**
  - Micro-labels, hygiene cert tags, and category chips must use uppercase letter-spaced `label-sm` or `label-md` to maintain structured architectural discipline.
  - Large display headlines should maintain tight negative letter tracking to retain optical weight against deep charcoal backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a clean fluid grid designed to display large high-resolution portfolio imagery without visual crowding.

- **Grid Systems:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of 1320px, 1.5rem gutters, and 3rem lateral margins.
  - **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 1.25rem gutters and 2rem page margins.
  - **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column layout with 1rem gutters and 1.25rem canvas margins.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace Philosophy:** Negative space is treated as a luxury asset. Section separations require generous vertical room (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem–6rem on desktop) to allow high-detail tattoo and piercing flash to breathe without competing with adjacent text blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, cloudy drop shadows; instead, it relies on tonal stacking, hairline borders, and warm ambient illumination.

- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Base level (`#0D0D11`) recedes into the infinite background.
  - Cards, consultation forms, and portfolio items sit on `#16161D`.
  - Floating controls, sticky navigation bars, and drawers sit on `#1E1E28`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Ghost Hairlines:** Surfaces are demarcated with subtle 1px outlines (`#2A2A38`). Active, featured, or VIP tiers transition their border to a low-opacity metallic gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Gold Ambient Glow:** Drop shadows are replaced with subtle, targeted backdrops:
  - Elevation 1 (Interactive Cards): `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`
  - Elevation 2 (Modals / Booking Sheets): `0 16px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`
- **Glass Surfaces:** Sticky navigation elements utilize backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with `rgba(13, 13, 17, 0.85)` surface opacity for seamless scrolling.

## Shapes

This design system employs a refined **Soft (`1`)** shape language. 

- **Corner Radii Hierarchy:**
  - Standard elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Chips): `0.25rem` (4px). Provides crisp geometric stability without clinical harshness.
  - Grouping Containers (Cards, Portfolios, Dialogue Boxes): `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px).
  - Media & Hero Gallery Elements: Maintained at `0.5rem` to avoid cutting off delicate perimeter lines of tattoo artwork.
  - Round Avatar and Status Indicators: Fully circular (`9999px`) for artist profile portraits and hygiene status dots.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Book Session, Deposit):** Solid gold fill (`#D4AF37`) with dark ink text (`#0D0D11`). Font is `Space Grotesk` semi-bold with subtle tracking. Hover state transitions to brighter champagne gold (`#E5B869`) with an elevated gold glow.
- **Secondary Action (Explore Flash, Meet Artists):** Deep charcoal fill (`#16161D`) with a 1px border (`#2A2A38`) and prominent text (`#F4F4F6`). Hover shifts border to gold (`#D4AF37`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost (View Details, Aftercare Guides):** Transparent background with muted gold text and an animated underline on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Artist Specialties & Styles:** `#1E1E28` surface with `#A6A6B4` body, 4px radius, padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Hygiene & Safety Credentials:** `#13221E` surface container paired with `#50B498` text and a green status indicator dot, signaling verified autoclaving, single-use needles, or certified piercers.

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- **Fields:** Dark background (`#16161D`) with a 1px boundary (`#2A2A38`). Placeholder text rests at `#686877`. Focused state sharpens with a 1px border of `#D4AF37` and no loud focus rings.
- **Custom Selectors (Date, Placement Selector, Piercing Jewelry Grade):** Segmented buttons built on `#0D0D11` trays with smooth sliding active tabs rendered in `#262634` bordered in `#D4AF37`.

### Cards & Galleries
- **Portfolio Cards:** Aspect ratios locked strictly to 4:5 or 1:1. Imagery maintains pure presentation without colored overlays until hover. On hover, a bottom-anchored scrim reveals artist credits, style tags, and booking availability.
- **Artist Profile Cards:** Features monochromatic or moody warm-toned portraits with newsreader-based display names, followed by a list of primary techniques and an instant consultation CTA.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox elements with 2px inner rounding. Unchecked: `#16161D` with `#2A2A38` border. Checked: `#D4AF37` fill with `#0D0D11` check icon. Used extensively in medical declaration and client consent forms.

### Studio-Specific Components
- **Hygiene Transparency Widget:** A dedicated block showcasing studio certification, sterilization logs, and jewelry materials (ASTM F-136 Titanium, 14k/18k Solid Gold).
- **Healing Stage Timeline:** A step-by-step progress component that guides clients through preparation, session expectations, and aftercare phases using fine metallic rules and clean typography.
